# ULTD TRANS PRO — Business Operations Architecture

ULTD TRANS PRO is an AI-native business operating layer, not a translation application.

Core domains in the current UX:
- Customer and company operations
- Deals and pipeline
- Activities and follow-up
- Finance and invoices
- Campaigns and outbound communications
- Executive reports and insights
- People and HR context
- AI agents, tasks, approvals, executions and audit
- Future integrations with AI providers, email, CRM, storage and automation tools

The long-term loop is:

Observe → Understand → Recommend → Approve → Execute → Verify → Audit → Learn

Industry-specific behavior should live in vertical packs, while the Core remains reusable.
